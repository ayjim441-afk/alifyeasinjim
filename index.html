About Me & My Passion for History

Student Profile & Academic Interests

I am an undergraduate student currently pursuing my B.A. (Honours) in Islamic History and Culture at Abudhar Giffari College, Dhaka.

For me, history is never just memorizing dry dates—it is about understanding real people, civilizational ideas, and how our culture evolved. I have a genuine fascination with Bengal's dynastic heritage, medieval architectural wonders, and South Asian social history.

Whether analyzing historical documents, writing structured academic essays, or exploring heritage landmarks in person, I approach my studies with curiosity, discipline, and honest dedication.
